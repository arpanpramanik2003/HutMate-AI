==================================================
MOTHERS HUT RESTAURANT - SUPPLIER PROCUREMENT GUIDELINES
==================================================

1. APPROVED VENDOR DIRECTORY & SCHEDULES

Supplier 1: ABC Foods Ltd
- Primary Category: Fresh Poultry, Meat & Eggs
- Delivery Schedule: Daily at 07:00 AM.
- Minimum Order Value: INR 3,000.
- Quality Check: Poultry must be delivered chilled (below 4°C). Temperature probe reading must be signed on the delivery challan. Any delivery above 5°C must be rejected immediately.

Supplier 2: Golden Grain Traders
- Primary Category: Basmati Rice, Grains, Cooking Oil, Flour & Spices
- Delivery Schedule: Tuesdays and Fridays between 02:00 PM and 05:00 PM.
- Minimum Order Value: INR 5,000.
- Packaging Standard: All rice bags must be sealed with moisture-proof food grade lining. Inspect for torn sacks or pest presence before signing invoices.

Supplier 3: Fresh Farm Produce
- Primary Category: Fresh Vegetables (Onions, Tomatoes, Potatoes, Herbs, Ginger, Garlic)
- Delivery Schedule: Mondays, Wednesdays, and Saturdays at 06:30 AM.
- Acceptance Standards: Firmness inspection on tomatoes; discard rate must not exceed 2% per crate.

2. PAYMENT TERMS & PURCHASE REQUEST APPROVAL LIMITS
- All draft purchase requests above INR 1,000 or exceeding 15 kg must be authorized by the Kitchen Head or General Manager.
- Emergency off-cycle orders incur a 10% delivery surcharge and must be flagged as urgent in the system.
