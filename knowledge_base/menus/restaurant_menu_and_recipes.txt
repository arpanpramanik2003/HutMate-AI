ITEM 1: DUM CHICKEN BIRYANI (SIGNATURE)
- Category: Main Course (Non-Vegetarian)
- Preparation Time: 45 minutes | Cooking Style: Slow Dum
- Standard Portion: Serves 2 (approx. 650g)
- Required Ingredients:
  * Chicken Breast / Bone-in cuts: 500g
  * Aged Basmati Rice: 400g
  * Plain Yogurt / Curd: 150g (for marinade)
  * Sliced Yellow Onions (Fried Birista): 120g
  * Ginger-Garlic Paste: 30g
  * Special Garam Masala Blend: 15g
  * Cooking Oil / Pure Ghee: 50ml
  * Fresh Mint & Coriander leaves: 25g
  * Saffron soaked in warm milk: 30ml
- Method:
  1. Marinate chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, garam masala, salt, and half the fried onions for at least 2 hours.
  2. Parboil basmati rice with whole spices (cardamom, cloves, bay leaf) until 70% cooked.
  3. Layer marinated chicken at the bottom of a heavy copper handi, cover with parboiled rice, top with saffron milk, mint, and remaining birista.
  4. Seal pot with dough lid and cook on gentle flame for 30 minutes.

ITEM 2: PANEER BUTTER MASALA (ROYAL)
- Category: Main Course (Vegetarian)
- Required Ingredients:
  * Fresh Cottage Cheese (Paneer cubes): 300g
  * Fresh Ripe Tomatoes (Pureed): 250g
  * Cashew Paste (Soaked & ground): 40g
  * Butter & Cream: 50g
  * Kitchen King / Garam Masala: 10g
  * Kasuri Methi (Fenugreek leaves): 5g
- Preparation Note: Simmer tomato and cashew gravy until oil separates. Fold in paneer cubes gently; do not boil paneer for more than 3 minutes to avoid rubbery texture.

ITEM 3: GARLIC BUTTER NAAN
- Ingredients: Refined flour (Maida), yogurt, baking powder, minced fresh garlic, melted butter, fresh coriander. Baked at 350°C in the clay tandoor.
