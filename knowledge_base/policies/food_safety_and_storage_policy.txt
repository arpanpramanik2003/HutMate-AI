==================================================
MOTHERS HUT RESTAURANT - STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES
DOCUMENT: Food Safety, Storage, and Hygiene Guidelines (Rev 2026.1)
==================================================

1. REFRIGERATION & STORAGE TEMPERATURE STANDARDS
- All raw poultry (chicken, turkey) must be stored at or below 4°C (39°F) on the lowest shelf of the walk-in refrigerator to prevent cross-contamination from drips.
- Cooked foods and ready-to-eat items must be kept on the upper shelves, strictly separated from raw items.
- Deep freezer temperatures must remain at or below -18°C (0°F) at all times. Temperatures must be logged twice daily (08:00 AM and 08:00 PM).
- Dairy products (milk, paneer, yogurt) must be stored between 2°C and 4°C and used within 5 days of opening.

2. FIFO (FIRST-IN, FIRST-OUT) INVENTORY ROTATION
- All incoming dry goods, produce, and meats must receive an intake date label upon delivery.
- Existing stock must always be moved forward, and new stock placed behind it.
- Expired or spoiled ingredients must be logged in the waste log and disposed of immediately.

3. KITCHEN HYGIENE & CROSS-CONTAMINATION CONTROLS
- Color-coded cutting board rules:
  * RED Board: Raw red meat and poultry only.
  * GREEN Board: Fresh produce and fruits only.
  * BLUE Board: Seafood and fish only.
  * YELLOW Board: Cooked poultry and cooked meats.
  * WHITE Board: Dairy, bakery, and bread.
- Handwashing is mandatory: before food prep, after touching raw poultry, after using the restroom, and after taking out trash.

4. ALLERGEN SAFETY PROTOCOLS
- Major allergens present in the kitchen: Dairy (ghee, butter, paneer), Tree Nuts (cashews, almonds in gravies), and Gluten (wheat flour/maida in rotis and naans).
- When an allergen-alert ticket is received from front-of-house, a sanitized pan and dedicated utensils must be used.
